Privacy Policy

Cadence : Speech Visualised · Last updated November 13, 2026

Cadence is a speech coaching app built by an individual developer. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

Data We Collect

Cadence does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our own servers. We have no backend, no analytics, and no user accounts. Everything you do in Cadence — practice sessions, passages, session history — stays on your device.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Cadence uses your device's microphone to analyze your speech in real time, generating the Speech Flow DNA visualization during live practice sessions. Speech transcription is powered by Apple's Speech framework. Depending on your device and settings, this transcription may be processed on-device or sent to Apple's servers for processing — this is handled entirely by Apple's system frameworks, not by Cadence or its developer. Apple's handling of this data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. Cadence itself does not store, transmit, or have access to your audio or transcriptions beyond your device.

Camera

If you use the optional "Record & Review" feature, Cadence accesses your device's camera to record video for your own review. This recording stays on your device and is never uploaded or shared by Cadence.

Face Data and Eye Contact Analysis

Cadence's optional eye contact feature uses the front camera and Apple's ARKit face tracking (which uses the TrueDepth camera on supported devices) to detect, in real time, whether you are looking toward the camera while you practice. The only information used is face tracking data such as head orientation and gaze direction.

Use: This data is used only to give you live eye contact feedback during your session. It is not used for identification, authentication, advertising, analytics, or profiling.

Storage and retention: Face data is processed in real time in your device's memory and is discarded immediately. Cadence does not save face images, face meshes, or face tracking data. At most, a simple summary result (such as how often you maintained eye contact) is saved in your session history on your device.

Sharing: Face data never leaves your device. Cadence does not send it to any server and does not share it with any third party. Cadence has no servers and uses no third-party SDKs.

Deletion: Because face data is not stored, there is nothing to delete. Session summaries saved on your device can be removed by deleting the session or uninstalling Cadence.

Data Sharing

Cadence does not share any data with third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers, because it does not collect any data in the first place.

Children's Privacy

Cadence does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, since no data leaves your device except as described above for Apple's speech recognition.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of Cadence adds an account system or analytics — this page will be updated, and the change will be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the GitHub Issues page for Cadence.
